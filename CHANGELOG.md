# Changelog

## 2.0.1

- Added: `CHANGELOG.md` to the package.

## 2.0.0

- Removed: `stylelint` less than `18.0.0` from peer dependencies.
- Removed: support for end-of-life and older versions of Node.js.
- Fixed: `value-keyword-layout-mappings` false positives for `offset-anchor` and `offset-position`.

## 1.0.0

- Removed: support for Stylelint less than `17.10.0`.
- Added: `*-layout-mappings: "flow-relative"`.
- Added: `relative-selector-nesting-notation: "implicit"`.
- Fixed: consistency of problem messages.

## 0.1.0

- Initial release.
